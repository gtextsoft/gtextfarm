Commercial Poultry Production Built for Volume and Freshness

A Dedicated Line for Meat Birds and Eggs

Gtext Poultry is the livestock arm of Gtext Farms. We raise broilers and layers under scheduled production cycles so buyers can plan weekly and monthly volumes without last-minute shortages.


Housing, feed, vaccination, and processing are coordinated as one system. That structure keeps birds healthy, eggs consistent, and deliveries predictable for markets, hotels, and institutions.

How the Houses Run

Poultry quality is decided in the house, not at the gate. These controls sit behind every dispatch.

01

Controlled Housing

Ventilation, stocking density, and litter management are kept within set ranges for bird comfort and growth.

02

Feed and Health

Ration schedules and vaccination routines reduce losses and keep product quality stable across batches.

03

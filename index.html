The company

Agriculture, run like production.

Gtext Farms, a subsidiary of Gtext Holdings, combines primary growing with processing and distribution. The point is not a harvest story. It is consistent volume, food safety, and delivery that buyers can plan around.

We work with growers, aggregators, processors, and logistics teams so product can be traced and moved without waste. That structure serves both retail shelves and high-volume institutional demand.

Our work is to build food systems that hold quality from the field to the crate.
